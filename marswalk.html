DATA BASIS & REFERENCES

Terrain is procedurally generated as an analog of Gale Crater / Mount Sharp (swap in a MOLA/HRSC/HiRISE DTM from JPL Mars Trek for real sites). Layers are modeled after: topography (MOLA, HiRISE DTMs), thermal (THEMIS, MSL REMS), radiation (MSL RAD ≈0.6 mSv/day surface GCR), geology (USGS-NASA Planetary Geologic Mapping), boxwork (Curiosity 2025).
